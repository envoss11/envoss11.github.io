> One thing is clear, the companies who will lead the way in this Agentic Analytics shift, will be those who have the foresight to build a strong data engineering foundation and invest in their semantic modeling

> With some fine-tuning, they can even outperform larger models on focused tasks.

> newer architectures now use an orchestrator agent that breaks tasks into smaller pieces and delegates them to specialized sub-agents.

> This “divide and conquer” approach also opens the door for sLMs to play a bigger role. Since each sub-agent only needs to handle a narrow task, even lightweight models can perform well when combined in a well-orchestrated system.

> Instead of training a new model for every metric or product, foundation models like TimeGPT are starting to handle forecasting, anomaly detection, and other analytic tasks straight from raw data
