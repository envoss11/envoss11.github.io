Reading the Hong translation. Part I is the aesthete "A"'s papers; Part II is
Judge William's letters back to him. Background: [[existentialism]].

## 2026-09-28 · Preface

- Victor Eremita "finds" the papers in a secretary desk. The pseudonymous frame
  matters: Kierkegaard does not want to be the authority on which life is right.
- The title is the point. The book poses a choice rather than arguing for one.

## 2026-10-05 · Diapsalmata

- Aphorisms of a bored, brilliant young man. Funny, then bleak.
- The aesthete lives for the interesting moment, and that strategy is already
  failing him. See [[the-aesthetic-stage]].

## 2026-10-12 · The Immediate Erotic Stages

- Long essay on Mozart's *Don Giovanni*. Music as the medium of pure sensuous
  immediacy, which language can only point at.

## 2026-10-19 · Rotation of Crops

- Boredom as the root of evil. The cure he proposes, arbitrarily varying how you
  look at things, is a technique for never committing to anything.
- Question for later: is this Pascal's diversion with the theology removed?

## 2026-10-23 · The Seducer's Diary

- Hard to read. The aesthetic life taken to its end: other people become
  material. Sets up Part II, and [[Fear and Trembling]] is the next step.
