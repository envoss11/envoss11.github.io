All of it in an industry where PII and PHI handling, HIPAA, and model governance are constraints on the design rather than paperwork added at the end. Although my professional focus these days is mostly on practical topics like document AI, agent architecture, and evaluation, I also like to dive into more off-the-wall research areas while exercising my "traditional" data science and statistical modeling muscles. Whatever comes of that ends up in posts — full write-ups when the work had a result worth showing, shorter notes when it didn't, and a few things still being figured out in the open.
