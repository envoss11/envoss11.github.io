# About

<img class="portrait" src="/assets/images/portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of Eric Voss" width="160" height="160">

I'm an AI engineer in financial services. I build document intelligence
platforms and decision support systems, mostly on AWS, and I care a lot about
measuring whether they actually work.

I'm a Lead Data Scientist in the AI Center of Excellence at
[Securian Financial](https://www.securian.com/), which covers life insurance,
annuities, and group benefits. In practice the role is AI engineering. I've
shipped a production generative AI pipeline for underwriting and medical
document extraction, and I'm now building out a document processing platform.

My career spans more than a decade across application development, analytics,
and AI/ML in the financial services industry. I have extensive experience in AI
development with the AWS ecosystem, including SageMaker, Bedrock, AgentCore,
and more. My previous experience also includes "traditional" data analysis and
predictive modeling for actuarial applications.
