**Placeholder page.** This one exists to show that the optional front matter really is
optional — with no `image`, `facts`, or `links` set, the layout drops those blocks and the
page still holds together.

Use this shape for smaller pieces of work that deserve their own page but do not have a
demo, a repo, or a hero chart to point at. It is still a post: it has a date, it sits in
the log, and it goes out on the feed. `featured: true` is the only thing putting it in the
card grid as well.
