**Placeholder post.** This one exists to show that an entry can be small. No
image, no tags, no headings: a title, a date, and a couple of paragraphs.

Half the point of keeping the log open to everything is that a short entry is
allowed to exist in it. Something I read, something that annoyed me, something I
want written down before I forget the shape of it — none of that needs a
write-up, and none of it has to be about data science.
